Project Overview

MyStoreNigeria is an end-to-end multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace designed to help customers discover quality products, shop from registered vendors, and manage their purchases in one place, while giving vendors the tools to manage their stores and orders.

Role
Product Designer
Platform
Web/web app
Tools

Industry
E-Commerce

The problem

Shopping from different vendors online can involve moving between platforms, comparing products across different stores, and figuring out which sellers to buy from. For vendors, reaching customers and managing their products and orders can also require a clear, organised system.

The goal of this project was to design a multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace that brings customers and registered vendors together in one platform. The experience needed to make product discovery and purchasing straightforward for customers while giving vendors and administrators the tools to manage their respective activities.

Three users. One system. Very different jobs

Customer

Wants
Find the right product quickly and buy with confidence.
Worries
Fake sellers, hidden costs, failed delivery.
Gets
Seller performance on every product, delivery and returns up front, visual search.

Vendor

Wants
Start selling fast and run the store with little effort.
Worries
Long setup, unclear fees, chasing couriers.
Gets
Guided onboarding, commission shown at listing, net earnings per order, platform delivery.

Admin

Wants
A trustworthy, profitable marketplace.
Worries
Fraudulent vendors, poor listings, payout errors.
Gets
Vendor and product review, order and revenue oversight.

How the market place connects

Customers, vendors, and administrators use different parts of the platform, but their activities are connected.

The design brings these experiences together while giving each user group the tools it needs to complete its tasks.

The user/customer experience

Browse & explore products on categories

Rather than having to navigate through an unstructured collection of products, customers can explore relevant categories and browse the available listings within each one.

This structure makes product discovery more organised, gives customers a clearer starting point, and helps them narrow their search towards items they are interested in purchasing.

Product details

Once a customer selects a product, the product details page provides a more comprehensive overview of the item. Key information, including product specifications, warranty details, and customer reviews, helps customers understand what they are considering before making a purchase.

The page also introduces the vendor behind the listing. Customers can explore the vendor's profile and browse other products they offer, creating an additional point of reference beyond the individual product.

Camera search. (Point, Scan & shop)

Sometimes, customers know exactly what a product looks like but don't know what to call it. Camera Search offers an alternative to text-based search, allowing users to find products by simply pointing their camera at an item or scanning an image.

Vendor dashboard

Overview

The vendor dashboard provides a central overview of store activity, helping vendors understand how their business is performing without having to navigate through multiple sections.

The four key metrics: revenue, total orders, listed products, and store views gives vendors a quick snapshot of their sales activity, order volume, product catalogue, and store visibility.

Vendor’s onboarding (The essential steps to set up their store).

The onboarding process guides vendors through the essential steps of setting up their store, submitting the required verification documents, and providing their payout details, ensuring they have the necessary information in place to operate on the marketplace.

Product Management

This gives vendors the tools to build and maintain their product catalogue. From adding new products to keeping track of listing limits, the interface is designed to make product management more organised, helping vendors maintain their store and keep their offerings up to date.

Earnings and wallet Management

The earnings and wallet section provides vendors with a central place to monitor their earnings and manage withdrawals. By bringing these activities together, the experience helps vendors keep track of their available funds and access the withdrawal process when they are ready to receive their earnings.

Inventory Management

Keeping track of stock is essential to managing a store effectively. The inventory management is designed to help vendors organise and monitor the stock of their listed products from one place. This gives vendors a more convenient way to keep inventory information up to date and stay informed about product availability, supporting better day-to-day store management.

Reflection & next steps

Designing this marketplace helped me understand the importance of creating connected experiences for customers, vendors, and administrators. With the product approaching launch, the next step is to gather real user feedback, evaluate how well the key journeys work, and identify areas for improvement.

Since the designs have not yet been validated through real-world use, post-launch testing will help me understand what works, what needs refinement, and how the experience can continue to evolve around users' needs.
